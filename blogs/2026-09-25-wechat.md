Hi Nina, Thank you for your question. At this time, WeChat Pay is not available as a native payment method in Mews Payments, and Mews does not currently offer a supported direct integration with WeChat Pay. A separate payment provider may be used outside the Mews Payments setup, but this would be a customer-managed third-party arrangement rather than a Mews-supported integration.
