Hi everyone Your guests tell your team useful things. “I’m gluten-free.” “It’s our anniversary.” “Please don’t knock before 10am.” Right now most of that disappears when the stay ends — so the guest repeats themselves next time, and your team re-enters it. We’re building a Guest Context Agent that changes this.
