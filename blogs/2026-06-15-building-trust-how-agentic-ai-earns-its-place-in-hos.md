This piece explores how agentic AI can build trust in hospitality operations through earned autonomy, where systems propose actions for human approval before gaining independent authority. It emphasizes transparency in decision-making, closed-loop learning from staff corrections, and maintaining brand consistency rather than just operational accuracy.
